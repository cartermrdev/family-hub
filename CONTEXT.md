# Family Hub

A shared command center for one household: one place to see and manage the family's calendars, chores, shopping lists, notes and messages, from a wall-mounted display and from each member's phone.

## Language

**Household**:
The single family the hub serves; everything in the hub belongs to it.
_Avoid_: Family account, tenant, home

**Member**:
A person in the Household who uses the hub; every Member is either an Adult or a Teen.
_Avoid_: User, family member, profile

**Adult**:
A Member who also manages the Household: its Members, Linked Calendars and Chore assignments.
_Avoid_: Parent, admin, owner

**Teen**:
A Member without Household-management rights, who can otherwise do everything an Adult can.
_Avoid_: Child, kid, restricted user

**Wall Display**:
The shared, always-on screen in a central spot in the house, which shows the dashboard and accepts touch input.
_Avoid_: Kiosk, tablet, hub screen

**Acting Member**:
The Member who tapped their avatar on the Wall Display, and so is credited with what is done there until the next tap.
_Avoid_: Current user, session, logged-in user

**Family Calendar**:
The Household's shared iCloud calendar, which Members can create and edit events on through the hub.
_Avoid_: Shared calendar, home calendar

**Linked Calendar**:
An external calendar (such as a Member's Google or work Outlook calendar) that the hub shows but never changes.
_Avoid_: Work calendar, external calendar, synced calendar

**Visibility**:
A Member's per-Linked-Calendar choice of showing Full Details or Busy Only.
_Avoid_: Privacy setting, sharing level

**Chore**:
A piece of household work, optionally repeating and optionally assigned to one Member; a one-off task is a Chore with no repeat.
_Avoid_: Task, to-do, job

**Shopping List**:
A named list of Items to buy, such as "Groceries" or "Costco".
_Avoid_: List, grocery list

**Item**:
One thing on a Shopping List, with an optional quantity and the Member who added it.
_Avoid_: Product, entry

**Note**:
A message pinned to the board for the whole Household, with no recipient.
_Avoid_: Sticky, post, announcement

**Message**:
Something one Member sends to one or more other Members, shown on the Wall Display and on each recipient's phone; it has no replies.
_Avoid_: Chat, note, DM
